## Findings

### W8-A-1 · P3 — "The monitored locations" is wider than the count's own filter

- **Reproduction:** `#instruments` prints "4 of the monitored locations have never had
  an instrument in them: MW01A, MW03B, MW11, MW12" (`screens.mjs:4753`). The
  computation (`seed.mjs:2915`) filters `LOCATIONS` to `klass === 'groundwater' ||
  'tsf_instrumentation'` — silently outside the sentence's scope is **SW01**, a
  monitored location (the licensed discharge point, DP01, quarterly return due) with no
  instrument. Under the sentence's own words the count is arguably 5.
- **Why P3, not P0:** no cross-screen contradiction, and the screen's context is
  downhole/barometric instrumentation, so the groundwater reading is the natural one —
  the sentence is imprecise, not false under its best reading. One word closes it.
- **Correction:** "monitored groundwater locations" (or "monitored bores and
  instrumented points"), or widen the filter and let SW01 appear with its own note.

### W8-A-2 · P3 — Two unrelated 0.38s in one story, unremarked

- **Reproduction:** the anomaly literal is `spike.metres = 0.38` (rendering as a
  computed 0.382 m swing on LS-01) and the sensor correction is 16.00 − 15.62 =
  0.380 m — near-identical magnitudes for two causally unrelated events on the same
  plate and the same screen. A reviewer primed by the correction may read the anomaly
  as its artefact (or vice versa); nothing on the screen heads that off.
- **Correction:** either separate the magnitudes in the seed (e.g. a 0.27 m spike) or
  add one clause where the two meet ("the anomaly's size is coincidental to the
  correction's shift — they share no cause"). Cosmetic either way; the arithmetic on
  both is verified above.
