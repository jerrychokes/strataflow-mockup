## 1. The correction to the plan — 7 of 8 walkable

**Independently confirmed, and the correction is sound.** I parsed the brief's own sentence
(`VENDOR_REQUIREMENTS.md:124`) on `→` and compared it term by term with the typed list in
`seed.mjs:12899`:

```
8 brief terms | 8 typed — all eight MATCH verbatim (including the en dash in 2021–2026)
expressible 3 · an equivalent 4 · not expressible 1
```

That is exactly the wave's claim. Each verdict re-measured against the record:

- **Groundwater** — `expressible`, and applied as *two* dimensions (matrix Water, location class
  groundwater) at steps 2 and 3. Honest, and the row says why.
- **Unit C** — `an equivalent`. `0` locations are in anything called Unit C; the dimension is held
  on every location, the value is not; the walk selects **Superficial** and says on the row that
  it is a substitution.
- **dissolved metals** — `an equivalent`. The suite is a size and not a membership, so what
  resolves is the 5 of 11 grid rows carrying the dissolved fraction. Named.
- **2021–2026** — `an equivalent`. 2 of the 6 years asked for hold no result (2021, 2022) and the
  grid is one round, so a period narrows nothing on it. Named, and the row says the population
  that *does* have a period is the 14-value series behind it.
- **validated results only** — `expressible`, empties the population (§2 below).
- **rejected data excluded** — `not expressible`. Quarantine is named as the nearest and
  distinguished: a held row was never in the population, so excluding it removes nothing.
- **comparison against nominated groundwater criteria** — `expressible`, 2 sets in force.

**On the equivalents specifically** — the thing to be hardest on: none is quietly substituted.
In every one of the four the substitution is stated on the row itself, in the `via` column, in
the same table cell a reader reads the verdict from. That is the honest form. One strains its
own definition — W20-A-3.

## Findings

### W20-A-1 · P2 — §4.3's eight terms are the wave's central claim and the only enumeration it did not close

- **Evidence.** §4.2's 43 and §4.4's 6 are compared with the brief word for word and in order.
  §4.3's eight terms are hand-typed at `seed.mjs:12899` and closed by nothing. Experiment: delete
  the `rejected data excluded` entry from the typed list and rebuild —

  ```
  EXPERIMENT 5 (drop 'rejected data excluded' from typed list) node exit=0
  ```

  The build stays green, `EXPLORER.counts.terms` silently becomes 7, `notExpressible` becomes 0,
  and every derived sentence — including `#coverage`'s "of its 8 terms … 1 … is not expressible
  at all" — re-reads itself into agreement. The wave's headline finding would delete without a
  murmur.
- **Affected criterion.** The wave's own stated principle, in the guard's own comment: "a screen
  that measured 43 things against a list of 43 things it typed itself would be checking its own
  homework". §4.3's list is typed, and it is the list the wave's central claim rests on.
- **Expected.** The eight terms parsed from `VENDOR_REQUIREMENTS.md:124` and compared with the
  typed list, in order, as §4.2 and §4.4 already are.
- **Observed.** No closure; the count is self-declared.
- **Not a falsehood today.** I verified all eight verbatim and the 3/4/1 split independently, so
  nothing on the screen is currently wrong. This is a guard gap, not a defect in the drawing —
  hence P2.
- **Correction.** Split the brief's sentence on `→` and run it through the same `inOrder(…)`
  helper. It is three lines beside the two that already exist.

### W20-A-2 · P2 — The guard is blind to a nested bullet, which its own comment says cannot happen

- **Evidence.** Adding one indented sub-bullet to §4.2 of the brief:

  ```
  - Fraction, including dissolved or total
    - Colloidal fraction          ← added
  EXPERIMENT 3 node exit=0        ← no offence reported
  ```

  Both the membership/order limb and the older count limb filter with `/^- /`, so an indented
  bullet is invisible to both and the dimension goes unmeasured.
- **Affected criterion.** The guard's stated property: "A dimension the brief adds cannot go
  unmeasured, one it drops cannot linger". The first half is false for nested bullets.
- **Expected.** A dimension added anywhere under §4.2 fails the build until it is measured.
- **Observed.** Added as a sub-bullet, it passes.
- **Severity.** P2 rather than P0: the brief as it stands has no nested bullets, so nothing is
  currently unmeasured, and the document is a fixed input. But this is the same shape as wave
  18's hole and it is cheap to close.
- **Correction.** Match `/^\s*- /` and record the indent, or fail the build on any indented
  bullet inside §4.2/§4.4 so the ambiguity has to be resolved deliberately.

### W20-A-3 · P3 — "arsenic and manganese" is counted as an equivalent, but nothing stands in for manganese

- **Evidence.** The wave defines its middle verdict as: "the record holds the dimension and not
  the value the brief names, **so the site's own value stands in and is named as a substitute**"
  (`seed.mjs:12877`). For this term the `via` reads "Arsenic resolves; manganese does not" and the
  `why` reads "No analyte in this dictionary is a manganese — 0 of 11 … a population that reported
  the arsenic count alone would be answering half the question as though it were the whole one".
  No substitute is named, because none exists. Arsenic is not a substitute for manganese — it is
  the other half of the same term, expressible as written.
- **Affected criterion.** "no dimension claimed as expressible that the record cannot express",
  and the aggregate sentence on `#coverage` ("4 are walked through the site's own equivalent and
  **named as substitutions**"), which is inaccurate for one of its four members.
- **Expected.** A term half of which has no record and no substitute is distinguishable from one
  where the site's own value genuinely stands in.
- **Observed.** Both are `an equivalent`, and the headline "7 of 8 walkable" counts this one as
  walked.
- **Why only P3.** Nothing is hidden: the row states plainly that manganese does not resolve and
  prints `0 of 11`, so a reader reaches the truth in the same cell as the verdict. This is a
  taxonomy that is one value short, not a false statement.
- **Correction.** Either a fourth verdict (`partly expressible`), or keep three and move this term
  to `not expressible` with the arsenic half noted — and adjust the aggregate sentence so
  "named as substitutions" is not claimed for a term that names none.

### W20-A-4 · P3 — The drawn control bar is a picture that responds when you operate it

- **Evidence.** `#explorer`'s bar is built from **7 enabled `<select>`s and 2 enabled text
  `<input>`s** — none `disabled`, `readonly`, or `aria-disabled`. Driving one:

  ```
  OPERATING A CONTROL: {"was":"Water (12)","now":"Soil (11)","displayChanged":true,"readoutChanged":false}
  ```

  The control visibly changes; nothing else on the screen moves. That is the one place the page's
  behaviour contradicts "a picture of a bar, not a bar".
- **Severity P3, deliberately.** This is catalogue-wide convention, not a wave regression: **39
  selects across the catalogue, 0 disabled**, on `search`, `locations`, `events`, `imports`,
  `field-capture`, `exceedances`, `crosstab` and more. The explorer is the *only* screen that
  states the limitation at all, and it states it three times. Raising it against this wave would
  punish the screen that is most honest about it.
- **Correction.** `disabled` (or `aria-disabled` plus `pointer-events: none`) on the drawn bar,
  which would make the sentence literally true rather than nearly true — and, if it is worth
  doing, catalogue-wide.

### W20-A-5 · P3 — The *Fraction* deferral is right, for a stronger reason than the one recorded

- **Judgement asked for, and my answer is: defer, do not rename here.** But the record understates
  where the defect lives.
- **Evidence.** `docs/GLOSSARY.md:1065` binds it: "**Dissolved**, not _filtered_: filtration is
  what was done, dissolved is what the number means", and line 900 gives the canonical form
  `Zinc (dissolved)`. `CLAUDE.md` binds UI strings to the glossary (G-06, QB-9). Every metal on
  this grid reads *(filtered)*. **But** the approved design oracle
  `design/reference/screens/results-crosstab.html` — from which this catalogue takes, in the
  README's own words, "the mark geometry, the result encoding, and **the cast**" — itself reads
  `Arsenic (filtered)` and `Cadmium (filtered)`.
- **So the conflict is between an approved oracle and the binding glossary**, and it lives in the
  app repo, not in this catalogue. Renaming here would put the mockup out of step with the oracle
  it is drawn from, and under GOALS §5 / `check-fixture-guard.mjs` an oracle change is its own
  goal and Jerry's to approve. Deferring is correct.
- **What is wrong with the record.** The dimension row (`seed.mjs:12807`) attributes it to this
  grid — "every analyte name on this grid says *(filtered)*" — and names neither the oracle nor
  the fact that the mockup is faithfully reproducing it. A reader of that note would conclude the
  fix belongs in this repository. It does not.
- **Correction.** Name the oracle in the note and route the decision to Jerry as an oracle
  question, so the deferral records the real blocker rather than a smaller one.
