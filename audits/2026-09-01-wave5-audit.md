  matches; seed `MEMBERS`, 0 matches). No directory group grants anyone Approver on
  MOCK-KRJ, so the binding every KRJ surface rests on is ungrounded on the one screen
  whose job is to ground it.
- **Affected criterion:** the wave's acceptance "no screen shows a count the seed
  contradicts" in its cross-screen-consistency spirit, and the audited claim that the
  bindings story "agrees between top bar, projects rows and #roles" — it agrees between
  the first two and is **absent** from the third. (`#roles` is deliberately outside the
  wave's seven screens; the inconsistency is nonetheless introduced by the wave, since
  before it there was no KRJ binding to ground.)
- **Expected:** a binding row (e.g., a KRJ operations group → Approver → MOCK-KRJ, with
  its member count) in seed `MEMBERS` and on `#roles`.
- **Observed:** `#roles` byte-identical to the parent build; no KRJ row anywhere on it.
- **Recommended correction:** a `#roles` rider in the next wave — one seeded group row
  granting Approver on MOCK-KRJ (A. Nakamura among its members), so the register backs
  what the top bar and project rows derive.
