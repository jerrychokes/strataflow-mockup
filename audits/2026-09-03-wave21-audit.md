  (retyping `prng`, the month generation and `ARSENIC_MW05` from `seed.mjs`, not importing

  2023-01 to 2026-04, values `5, 5, 6.7, 9.6, 9.2, 10.9, 13.4, 16.1, 16.5, 18.5, 20, 22.6,
  24.9, 26.1`. Not one of the table's six dates is in the series, not one of its detected
  values is a series value, the count is 6 against 14, and the LOR step is placed at
  2025-05 against the plate's step after the second point.
- **Expected** (wave constraint, verbatim): "nothing asserted that the record does not
  support". **Observed:** a caption asserting these are the plotted values, in a panel whose
  own text says it exists because "it is how somebody checks a value they are about to put

  not touch: D13 gates every figure family". That covers two. This one is in **`screens.mjs`

  "under a caption that calls them the plotted values") and documented it on a **different
  screen**. `#hydrograph` does link to `#statistics` in its "From here" rail, but nothing on
  or beside the table signals that it is wrong, so a reader using it for the stated purpose
  has no reason to click.
- **Correction:** re-caption the table for what it is, or withdraw it with its before
  recorded the way the two hydrochem sentences were withdrawn on the same commit. The
  wave already owns the pattern; it is applied twelve lines away on another screen.

### W21-A-2 · P1 — Two figure descriptions render into `#hydrochem` asserting exactly what the screen withdraws, and the screen does not name them

- **Reproduction:** text-extract `index.html` for `Ca-HCO3` and `four times the ionic`;
  `grep -n` in `figures.mjs` (lines 769 and 832). Rendered into `#hydrochem`:
  - Piper: *"MW05 plots as a distinctly sulfate-dominated water separate from **the Ca-HCO3
    background of every other bore**"*

    sulfate-dominated"*
- **Observed against the same screen's own measurements**, which I recomputed and confirm:
  1 of 6 bores holds a majority cation; at **4 of 6 the largest cation is not calcium**
  (MW03B, MW05, MW07, MW09); MW05 is **2.73× the mean** of the other five, spanning
  1.94×–3.84×. The screen prints the headline stat **"0 water types assigned"** and states
  in terms **"Four times every other bore is true of none of them."** So the screen asserts
  and refutes the same two propositions, and for a reader on the accessible-description path
  the refutation is not adjacent to the assertion.
- **Why this is a finding rather than an accepted fence.** I judge naming-and-not-changing
  **correct for the plate** — D13 gates the family, an approved plate is not a wave's to
  redraw, and editing a `<desc>` to match a measurement is exactly the "rendering that
  quietly improves on the thing it is measured against" the repository refuses elsewhere.
  But the fence governs *changing* the file, not *leaving the contradiction unnamed*. The
  wave built the instrument for this on the same commit — the five-readings table names two
  fenced `figures.mjs` literals on the face, with a build guard behind them — and did not
  apply it to the facies case on the screen the facies work landed on. `#hydrochem` contains
  **zero** occurrences of "figures.mjs" or of any reference to the plate descriptions.
- **Correction:** name both `<desc>` strings on `#hydrochem` as the arsenic readings are
  named on `#statistics` — quoted, dated, with the measurement beside them and the fence
  given as the reason they stand. No figure work required.

### W21-A-4 · P2 — The dominance count is reported clean where it turns on 0.22 of a point

- **Reproduction:** recomputed from the six `MAJOR_IONS` literals retyped from
  `seed.mjs:1404–1409`. MW09: Ca **35.79%**, Na+K **36.01%** — a **0.217-point** margin
  decides whether the count is 4 or 3. Under the split convention (Na and K separate) the
  count is **3 of 6**.
- **On the convention:** grouping Na+K is the Piper convention, it is the correct
  instrument, and it is applied consistently — the code groups it in one place
  (`cationShare`), the per-bore table annotates the cell "36.0% **with K**", and the
  comment states it is "what the Piper plate already does and is not a choice made here."
  No finding there.
- **On the margin:** the screen states no margin anywhere — I searched the rendered
  `#hydrochem` for `margin`, `marginal`, `0.22`, `narrowly`, `turns on`: **none**. A reader
  can recover it only by cross-reading two cells of a 42-row table and subtracting. Ion
  analyses carry measurement uncertainty far exceeding 0.2 points, so "1 of 6 holds a
  majority" is robust (26-point margin) while "4 of 6" is not reproducible against a
  re-analysis. **The same screen prints "worst +0.61%" for the ionic balance**, so the
  convention of printing the figure that tells you whether to believe the number exists on
  this screen and is not applied to its own headline count.
- **Correction:** state the margin where the count is stated — one clause, e.g. "at MW09 by
  0.2 of a point". The withdrawal itself does not depend on it: the withdrawn sentence fails
  on the majority half regardless.
