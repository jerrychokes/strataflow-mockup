## Findings

### W1-A-1 · P1 — The stacked-cell label fix trades overflow for superimposed, illegible text on ~30 screens

- **Evidence:** screenshots `375-dqa-consequence-cell.png` ("Consequ**Cadm**ence…" — label and
  value glyphs drawn over each other) and `375-qc-finding-cells.png` ("FindirLOR 1.0 µg/L…",
  "FindiiZinc recovered 62%…") vs `375-qc-finding-cells-prewave.png` (same cells at
  `f00308e`, fully legible). Measured: on the Wave 1 build at 375 px, **85 stacked-cell
  labels across ~30 screens** (dqa, qc, import-commit, certificate, qualifiers, batches,
  result-detail, signoff, supersession, coverage, …) render in a `::before` box narrower
  than the label's longest word ("Consequence" needs 82 px, gets 32–36 px; "Finding" needs
  46 px, gets 10–33 px), so the word overprints the value text. The same probe on the
  pre-wave build reports **0** (916 labelled cells in both builds; 0 shrinkable before,
  916 after).
- **Cause:** the Wave 1 rule `@media (max-width: 767px) { .sf-table--records tbody
  td::before { flex-shrink: 1; min-width: 0; } }` (chrome.mjs). `min-width: 0` permits
  shrink *below min-content*, and overflowing glyphs from a shrunk box extend neither
  `td.scrollWidth` nor the document's — which is why `verify.mjs` and the build stay green
  over it.
- **Affected acceptance criterion:** the wave's own method statement — "the fix is
  containment …, **never removal**" — and its objective ("the catalogue stops contradicting
  its own measurements"): the sweep now reports clean screens that are visibly broken.
- **Expected:** labels legible beside their values at 375 px, document overflow 0.
- **Observed:** document overflow 0, labels superimposed on values wherever a row is tight.
- **Recommended correction:** drop `min-width: 0` from that rule and keep `flex-shrink: 1`
  (flex's default `min-width: auto` floors the shrink at the longest word, and the label
  wraps). Validated by the auditor by injection: **0 screens overflow at 375 and collisions
  drop 85 → 3** (three "Re-run by" cells on `#coverage` that need their own look — some
  other constraint holds them at 32–38 px against a 42 px word). Then extend `verify.mjs`
  with the check that would have caught this: for every labelled stacked cell, the
  `::before`'s used width may not be narrower than its longest word.

### W1-A-2 · P1 — The coverage screen still prints a 23 August state claim that the register contradicts

- **Evidence:** `screens.mjs:4143`, rendered into `#coverage`: *"The state dots in the rail
  are the other half of the honesty: most of what is drawn here is tested library code with
  no route, and four screens are proposals against no requirement at all."* The register's
  `now` (which those same rail dots render) says **58 of 66 shipped, 0 engine-only, 6
  proposed** — found by walking every text node of every screen body in the rendered
  document against the build's own banned-phrase list, which the build applies only to
  `route:` lines.
- **Affected acceptance criterion:** "no screen prints a state that disagrees with the
  register" — verbatim from the Wave 1 acceptance list.
- **Expected:** every state assertion on a screen body agrees with the register or is
  explicitly dated as the 23 August record.
- **Observed:** a present-tense sentence describing the rail dots as showing what they no
  longer show, on the one screen whose job is honesty about coverage.
- **Recommended correction:** generate the sentence from register data (the counts exist in
  `build.mjs` already), or date it as the 23 Aug record; and widen the build's banned-phrase
  check from `route:` lines to screen body prose so the class of rot stays machine-caught.

### W1-A-3 · P2 — At 375 px, activating any screen leaves its content ~1185 px below the fold

- **Evidence:** measured (`mobile-nav-check` script): reading `#hardness` at scrollY 1185 and
  clicking its "From here → Result detail" link lands at scrollY 0 with the destination's top
  at **1185 px** in an 812 px viewport — the reader is deposited at the rail, not the content.
  At 1280 px the same click shows the destination at 197 px. Screenshot:
  `viewport375-after-activate-hardness.png` (post-activation viewport contains only the rail).
- **Cause:** at ≤900 px `.mk-app` collapses to one column with the 100 vh rail stacked first,
  and `show()` unconditionally `window.scrollTo({top: 0})`. Pre-existing — the Wave 1 diff
  touches neither rule — so this is filed against the surface, not the wave.
- **Affected criterion:** brief §10's "a walk of the wave's journey in the rendered output,
  at both desktop and 375 px" — the walk works but every hop lands on the rail.
- **Expected:** navigation brings the activated screen into view at every width.
- **Observed:** it does at 1280; at 375 it never does.
- **Recommended correction:** in `show()`, scroll the activated section into view when the
  layout is single-column (or collapse the rail into a drawer below 900 px).

### W1-A-5 · P2 — Checkbox and switch patterns miss the 44 px coarse-pointer floor

- **Evidence:** under emulated coarse pointer (`matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)')` = true) the
  tap target for `label.mk-check` runs as small as **16 px** tall and `label.mk-switch` is
  **27 px**; `chrome.mjs`'s `@media (pointer: coarse)` rules floor buttons, tabs, inputs,
  selects and icon buttons at 44 px but omit check/radio/switch. These are form controls,
  not the README's stated inline-link exception. Pre-existing (rules unchanged by the wave).
- **Affected criterion:** §10 touch targets; the mockup's own F-23 answer.
- **Expected:** ≥44 px effective target for form controls under a coarse pointer.
- **Observed:** 16–27 px on the check/switch patterns.
- **Recommended correction:** add `.mk-check`, `.mk-radio`, `.mk-switch` to the coarse-pointer
  `min-height: 44px` set; assert it in the verify.mjs touch check (W1-A-4).

### W1-A-9 · P3 — Every generated figure carries `aria-labelledby=""`

- **Evidence:** `figures.mjs:143` (`svgOpen`) hardcodes `role="img" aria-labelledby=""` on
  all 11 figures. An empty reference list is ignored by accessible-name computation, so the
  `<title>` child still names the figure in practice — but the attribute is dead markup that
  reads as a broken reference. Pre-existing.
- **Recommended correction:** drop the attribute, or point it at real `<title>`/`<desc>` ids.

## Verdict

**Wave 1 does not yet meet its acceptance criteria.** Blocking findings: **W1-A-1** (the

≤767 px — the acceptance is met on the meter and lost on the page) and **W1-A-2** (the

this audit; under §11 the wave closes when they land and re-verify. Everything else the
wave claimed was independently reproduced: zero overflow at three widths, panning with a
pinned identity column, state-claim-free route lines, the recorded stop-gate decisions,
and a build whose output is byte-identical to what is committed.
