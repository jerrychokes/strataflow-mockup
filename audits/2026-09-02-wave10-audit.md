### W10-A-1 · P1 — Three "established" mapping rows fail the tier's own definition, and the app repo's evidence contradicts two of them

- **Evidence:** the rendered tier legend defines Established as *"A source states the
  equivalence, and the export writes it"*, and the README's wave-10 section claims all
  *"14 land on a mapping this project already evidenced"*. Against the app repo's own
  ELDF-4 record (`docs/reference/esdat-edd-spec.md`):
  - **`ChemName`** — the Chemistry file has `ChemCode` + `OriginalChemName` (spec :90,
    :110); no field named `ChemName` exists. The row's basis cites "the ELDF-4 definition
    this instance already reads" — which is the mockup's own five-row illustration on
    `#formats`, inherited from 23 August, not a source.
  - **`Units`** — the Chemistry file's unit field is `Result_Unit` ("Distinct from
    `Detection_Limit_Units`", spec :112); `Units` is not among the 30 published columns.
    The row's basis names no source at all.
  - **`validation_state`** — no such column in the Chemistry (30) or Sample (12) lists;
    the name is this instance's own domain column. The basis ("the field the held rows
    are held on") names the domain side, not a target field — and the wave also writes
    the claim inbound, in the new ESdat inventory record's *preserves* sentence
    ("…and a validation-state field", `seed.mjs:6773`), which the spec's column lists
    contradict.
- **Affected acceptance criterion:** "the outward mapping stated … the mapping table is
  the ADR rendered", with the tiers as the evidence discipline; the README's
  all-14-evidenced claim.
- **Expected:** every Established row backed by a citable source, as eleven of the
  fourteen are. **Observed:** three rows carrying the source-stated badge with no stating
  source, two of them contradicted by the spec their neighbouring rows cite.
- **Recommended correction:** cite the real fields (`OriginalChemName` — with `ChemCode`
  noted as the paired key — and `Result_Unit`), and either ground `validation_state` in a
  named receiving-spec clause or move it to Refused with the honest basis that no
  published field carries it (both inbound and on the outward row). The tier counts,
  stats and prose all derive from the register, so nothing typed needs chasing.
