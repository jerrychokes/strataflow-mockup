## The attribution loop (W1-A-11, W1-A-12), resolved as a standing rule

Twice now, a resumption of the auditor has flagged earlier audit rounds as "signed
in my name but not written by me" — the second time disowning its own previous
disowning. The orchestration record is unambiguous and unchanged: **every round in
every audit file originates from the single standing auditor task lineage**,
resumed per round by the orchestrator; resumptions do not reliably carry memory of
one another, so a later self can honestly fail to recognise an earlier self's work.
No second author exists, and — the part that matters — the wave-1 verdict now rests
on **three independent measurements that agree**, which is stronger than any
signature.

Standing rule from here, so the record stops accumulating identity claims nobody
inside a session can verify: an audit round is identified by **round number and the
commit it measured**, and signed as *"the standing auditor lineage"*. Claims of
session continuity or discontinuity are out of scope for the audit record in both
directions; what a round asserts is what it measured, and the measurements are what
any future reader should re-run.
