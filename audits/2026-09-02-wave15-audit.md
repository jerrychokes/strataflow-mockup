## Findings

### W15-A-1 · P0 — The rollback card types the numbers the settlement just proved false, on the screen that proves them false

- **Evidence:** screenshot `w15-1280-criteria-rollback.png` and the rendered HTML: on
  `#criteria`, the activation panel's reversibility sentence reads *"Rolling back
  re-evaluates every total the rule reached — **1** — and withdraws whatever exceedances
  it raised, which on this round is **0**"* — and the Rollback card directly beneath it
  reads *"Derived totals re-evaluated: **7**"* and *"Exceedances withdrawn: **6** —
  withdrawn, never deleted"*. Source: `CRITERIA_DRAFT.rollback.writes` (seed.mjs, the
  `n: '7'` and `n: '6 — …'` literals) — typed, carrying no `was`/`typed` annotation,
  while every row of the activation card above them was converted to computed values
  with the typed history kept beside them.
- **Why it is a P0:** a falsehood *and* a self-contradiction on a drawn screen. The
  record holds one derived PFAS total; 7 was wrong even before this wave (it dates from
  before MW11's column was blanked — the exact defect the wave's own comment block
  documents for the activation preview and fixes there); 6 exceedances withdrawn is
  impossible against an activation the same screen shows raising 0. The wave's
  acceptance — *"every moved count recounted"* — missed this consumer.
- **Expected:** rollback's blast radius derived from the same counts as activation
  (`NON_DETECT.active.length`, the computed changes), with any before stated beside it
  as the activation card states its own.
- **Recommended correction:** replace the two literals with the computed values (1 and
  0 on this round), carrying `was`/`typed` the way `activation.writes` already does;
  re-run the build and this table.

### W15-A-2 · P3 — "Three more bottles" lost its qualifier on the way to the screen

- **Evidence:** the withdrawal panel on `#batches` renders *"The alternative reading —
  that the analyses happened and the manifest, the chain and the receipt all
  under-record them — would have to be drawn as three more bottles, a second consignment
  and a second receipt that nobody signed"*, two paragraphs after pricing **five** more
  bottles (38 → 43). The seed's own reasoning is coherent — three bottles is the cost of
  justifying **the batch's summary row of 4**, the strongest false reading ("Inventing
  three more bottles … *to justify a summary row*", `PFAS_REACH`'s doc block) — but the
  rendered sentence dropped that qualifier, and against "these 5 cells" in the same
  panel the 3-vs-5 reads as an unexplained inconsistency rather than as two different
  alternatives priced.
- **Expected:** the sentence binds its number to its alternative.
- **Recommended correction:** restore the qualifier ("to justify the batch's summary
  row") or compute the bottle count per reading (3 for the batch's 4, 5 for the grid's
  6).
