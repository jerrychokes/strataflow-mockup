## Findings

### W6-A-1 · P1 — The MW11 contradiction is real, live, and — contrary to the wave's claim — recorded nowhere in the repository

- **Reproduction:** render #crosstab and read the MW11 column: eleven populated,
  evaluated cells (pH 7.31 · EC 910 · arsenic 2.8 "assessed against this criterion and
  below it" · zinc 2.8 · sulfate 121 · …). On the same build, #field-capture's summary,
  session and preflight say MW11 was visited twice, found **dry**, yielded **0 samples**
  ("Dry locations recorded as attempted rather than missed: 1"), and home says "MW11 not
  sampled for 2026 Q2". Meanwhile #data-states states: *"The crosstab below is complete
  for every other location, and the MW11 column is **drawn empty** rather than omitted"*
  — measurably false against the rendered crosstab.
- **Provenance:** both sides predate the wave (`git log -S` puts the crosstab cells and
  the "drawn empty" sentence in the initial commit). The wave declared this
  known-and-stated: "crosstab's MW11 column contradicting the manifest is **recorded**
  as pre-existing for a later wave". The brief for this round says such items are not
  re-raised *unless the statement is wrong* — and the statement is wrong in its load-
  bearing half: `grep` across `seed.mjs`, `screens.mjs`, `README.md` and `audits/*.md`
  finds **no record** of the contradiction or the deferral anywhere in the repository.
  The only trace is the commit message. The lineage's qualifier-channel note shows what
  "recorded" is supposed to look like in this project; MW11 got no equivalent.
- **Why P1 rather than P0:** the drawing defect is pre-existing, not this wave's
  regression — but the wave's own handling of it (the record) did not land, its new
  field-record surfaces widen the live cross-screen contradiction from one screen to
  four, and a sentence on a drawn screen now asserts the opposite of what another drawn
  screen shows. Under the gate it blocks either way.
- **Correction:** write the record where the project keeps records — a comment beside
  `CROSSTAB` in `seed.mjs` and a visible note (crosstab or data-states) naming the
  contradiction and the wave that owns it — and fix the #data-states sentence to
  describe what is actually drawn (or make the drawing match the sentence by blanking
  the column, which is what its own text argues for). Any of these closes the finding;
  what cannot stand is a false sentence plus an unrecorded deferral.

  condition → pump…", and the wave plan's own scope line repeats "SWL → condition →
